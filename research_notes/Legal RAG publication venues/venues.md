# Publication venues for a faithful, citation-grounded legal RAG system (Indian compliance)

Research date: 2026-11-21. Many publisher pages (Elsevier ScienceDirect, SpringerLink) blocked automated fetching (403 or login redirects), so several figures come from search-result snippets of official pages or from third-party aggregators. Aggregator figures are marked "(aggregator, approx.)". Verify every metric on the live journal page and in Clarivate's Master Journal List before submitting.

## Q1. Which journals fit, and what are their indexing, speed, cost and realism?

### Takeaway
The best-fitting reputable journal is Springer's *Artificial Intelligence and Law* (subscription route with no fee, IF about 3.1, very fast desk triage). Realistic fast OA fallbacks are IEEE Access (binary review, 4 to 6 weeks to publication, about $2,000 or more APC), Springer *Discover Artificial Intelligence* (Scopus Q1, median 33 days to first decision, APC about $1,300 to $1,700), and PeerJ Computer Science. MDPI *Applied Sciences* is the fastest (about 15 to 20 days to first decision) but carries a lower reputation. *Computer Law & Security Review* fits a law-and-tech framing with high citation metrics, but it expects legal and policy depth beyond a pure system paper.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **Proposed ranked shortlist** (my synthesis, balancing fit, reputation and the 3-day timeline):
  1. *Artificial Intelligence and Law* (Springer): best fit and prestige, with a free subscription route. Expect months to a final decision.
  2. *IEEE Access*: fast binary review that suits applied system papers; APC about $2,000 to $2,800.
  3. *Discover Artificial Intelligence*: Scopus Q1, moderate APC, friendly to students.
  4. *Expert Systems with Applications* (Elsevier): suits applied systems, but desk rejection is common (not verified here).
  5. *Information Processing & Management* (Elsevier): RAG and IR faithfulness fit, but a high bar (not verified here).
  6. *Computer Law & Security Review*: only if the paper adds substantial Indian DPDP legal analysis.
  7. *PeerJ Computer Science*: rigour-only review.
  8. *Natural Language Processing Journal* (Elsevier): good topical fit; indexing needs checking.
  9. *Engineering Applications of AI* and *Knowledge-Based Systems*: high bar (not verified here).
  10. *Applied Sciences* or MDPI *AI* / *Information*: fastest, lower prestige.
  11. *International Journal of Law and Information Technology* (OUP) or T&F *ICT Law*: law-oriented and would need reframing.
  12. *ACM TALLIP*: Asian-language focus, so the fit is weak unless the paper covers Indian-language handling.
- Authors should show that the 16.8% unsupported-claim rate of the LLM-only baseline falls to about 0 to 0.5% with RAG, and include judge-validation (human agreement) evidence. Reviewers at AI & Law and IP&M will question LLM-judge reliability.
- A subscription (non-OA) route at Springer or Elsevier costs nothing. This matters for student authors.

## Q2. Which conferences and workshops fit, with deadlines in late 2026 to mid 2027?

### Takeaway
JURIX 2026, NLLP 2026 and FIRE 2026 deadlines have all passed. The next realistic targets are ACL 2027 via ARR (deadline 4 January 2027), ICAIL 2027 (deadline reportedly 28 January 2027, Vienna, July 2027), and the NLLP 2027 and JURIX 2027 cycles (expected August to September 2027, not yet announced).

### Cited Findings

The cycle is closed. — [JURIX CfP](https://jurix.nl/?p=705); [JURIX Google group](https://groups.google.com/g/jurix-foundation/c/uQ7SbNzliyc); [beri.net](https://www.beri.net/events/jurix-2026)

Long papers allow up to 8 pages, submission is via OpenReview, and proceedings appear in the ACL Anthology. The cycle is closed, and NLLP 2027 is likely in a similar August window. — [ACL portal NLLP 2026](https://www.aclweb.org/portal/content/nllp-2026); [JURIX group post](https://groups.google.com/g/jurix-foundation/c/hBSN5AEt_so)
- **ICAIL 2027:** paper and demo deadline 28 January 2027, notification 19 April 2027, workshop proposals 11 December 2026. Vienna, 5 to 9 July 2027. These dates come from aggregators and are unconfirmed on an official site. — [myhuiban ICAIL](https://www.myhuiban.com/conference/5671?lang=en-us); [beri.net ICAIL 2027](https://www.beri.net/events/icail-2027); [IAAIL host call](https://iaail.org/call-for-expressions-of-interest-to-host-icail-2027/)

### Inferences
- ICAIL 2027 (late January) is the strongest conference target after a journal submission. It accepts short papers and demos, which suits a system paper. Note that most journals forbid concurrent submission of the same work, so the authors must choose one or the other or make the versions substantially different.
- ACL/ARR on 4 January 2027 is possible, but main-track acceptance for an applied system with a 100-query eval is unlikely. A findings or demo track is more plausible.

## Q3. Which predatory or delisted journals should the authors avoid, and what is realistic in 3 days?

### Takeaway
Use Clarivate's Master Journal List and Scopus Sources to check any journal before submitting. Clarivate removed about 128 journals in 2025 and runs monthly removals in 2026, Cureus being a notable case. In 3 days, a paper can realistically be *submitted*, not accepted. Expect desk decisions within 1 to 5 weeks and full decisions in 1 to 4 months or more.

### Cited Findings
- Clarivate removed 128 journals from the WoS Core Collection in 2025 (mostly ESCI, then SCIE). The reasons are editorial de-listing, production de-listing and cessation. This comes from a secondary blog. — [PhDTalks 2025 list](https://phdtalks.org/2026/01/clarivate-removed-web-of-science-journals-list-2025.html); [WIUT library PDF](https://researchlrc.wiut.uz/media/attachments/2026/01/19/list-of-removed-journals-wos-2025.pdf)
- Cureus was removed from the Master Journal List in October 2025 and gets no JIF in the June 2026 JCR. — [Retraction Watch](https://retractionwatch.com/category/impact-factor); [CASRAI](https://casrai.org/news/cureus-web-of-science-delisting-2025)
- 2026 monthly removals were recorded in March, May, July (6) and October (7). — [PhDTalks July 2026](https://phdtalks.org/2026/07/web-of-science-journals-removed-july.html); [PhDTalks Oct 2026](https://phdtalks.org/2026/10/web-of-science-removes-seven-journals-october.html); [CASRAI delisting guide](https://casrai.org/guides/why-journals-get-delisted-from-web-of-science)
- Timeline benchmarks: IEEE Access takes 4 to 6 weeks to publication; Discover AI takes a median of 33 days to first decision; AI & Law a median of 6 days; Applied Sciences about 15 to 20 days; PeerJ CS a median of 148 days to acceptance. — sources as in Q1.

### Inferences
- Avoid unsolicited "fast publication, Scopus-indexed" journals that email students, "special issue" mills, and any journal missing from the current Master Journal List. Check against the PhDTalks and WIUT lists.
- A 3-day plan should cover a final paper in the journal's template, a cover letter on the faithfulness contribution, a data and code availability statement, a disclosure of LLM-judge use, and suggested reviewers.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the specific titles of journals in the 2026 removal lists. Check the linked lists for any candidate journal.
- I did not check Beall's-style predatory lists or UGC-CARE status, which is relevant for Indian academic credit.
